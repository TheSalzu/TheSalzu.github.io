Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its developer. It isn't offered to anyone else, so the only person whose data it handles is the developer.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail: new messages in the developer's own inboxes, to sort out urgent and school email and to find dates mentioned in it. When the developer asks, it also marks a message read or saves a reply draft. It never sends email without the developer's approval of that exact message.
  • Google Drive: only files Jarvis itself creates there. It can't see the developer's other files.
  • Google Calendar: only the "Jarvis" calendar the app creates. It can't see the developer's other calendars.
  • Google Tasks: the "Jarvis" task list, to list work that's still due.

How the data is used and stored

  • Everything Jarvis keeps (sign-in tokens, mail notes, due dates) stays on the developer's own PC. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted to the developer's Windows account.
  • To sort new email and find dates in it, Jarvis sends the text of those messages to Anthropic's Claude API, which processes it and returns the result. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • Jarvis doesn't sell, share or publish any data, and doesn't use it for ads.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on the PC deletes everything it stored.
