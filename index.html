Jarvis

A personal assistant app for one student. It isn't offered to anyone else.

Jarvis is a Windows desktop app its developer built for their own use. It keeps track of school work: it reads due dates and grades from the developer's school websites, puts the due dates on a "Jarvis" calendar and task list in the developer's own Google account, and points out school email that needs attention.

Jarvis signs in with Google only to the developer's own accounts, to:

  • read the developer's Gmail, to notice school and urgent email and dates mentioned in it (and, when asked, mark messages read or save reply drafts);
  • save files it makes into its own folder in Google Drive (it can't see other files);
  • create and update its own "Jarvis" calendar in Google Calendar (it can't see or change other calendars);
  • keep a "Jarvis" task list in Google Tasks with the work that's still due.
